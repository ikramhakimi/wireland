## Risks on the current page (compliance flags)

- Hero and hair-cycle cards name prescription medicines (Finasteride 1 mg, Minoxidil 5%) on a public page —
  `directions.md` §5 flags this.
- "New growth often shows within 4–8 months" is an outcome claim with no cited source.
- Product reviews ("Daniel L." etc.), "Raul Gonzalez" and the before/after cycles look like demo content.
  If they ship, they must be real and consented; patient testimonials for medical services need review.
- "MMC No. 00000" placeholders.
